Description
What Could You Offer Online Today? is a free, no-signup 60-second picker from SmartPickShop Holdings. Visitors choose the kind of task that feels easiest, who they would rather help, and how much time they have. The tool returns one practical, scoped digital-service idea, a suggested first step, and a message they can adapt. Picker choices and generated results stay in the visitor's browser. The tool does not promise earnings, collect an email, or force account creation.
Origin story
Most “make money online” advice creates another problem: too many options. I built this tool to reduce a giant list of possibilities to one small, concrete service idea someone can evaluate and test. The goal is not to promise income. It is to make the first decision easier and give the visitor a practical starting point.
