F.S.A. Account Activation

Use this page when an F.S.A. invitation or password-reset email opens the wrong destination.

Your full password is never sent to the breach-check service. F.S.A. sends only the first five characters of its SHA-1 hash and compares the returned suffixes locally. This page accepts only links issued by the F.S.A. Supabase project.
